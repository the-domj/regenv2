How many units are in your facility?

We have the 48 unit system mounted on racks at the corners of the room. 12 in each corner and laser measured to create a perfect toroidal field in the middle of the room.

How does the EE System work?

The EE-system works with 'Bio-scalar photon fusion technology' to generate multiple bio-active life-enhancing energy fields, including "scalar waves," which can allow cell regeneration, improve immune function, provide relief from pain, detoxify the body, elevate moods, and increase energy levels.

How many chairs do you have?

In our 1600 sq ft EE room, we have 25 comfortable "lazyboy" style manual reclining chairs in a theatre style seating arrangement. We also offer sleeping cots for overnights or those unable to sit on the recliners, per request. Our facility is ADA compliant.

How many hours is a first session?

The amount of hours is up to you. The general recommendation from Dr. Sandra is 2 hours as a starting point, but you are welcome to choose any length of time during the day or 10 hours at our overnight sessions.

How much does it cost, and do you offer memberships?

We offer hourly rates, affordable memberships (monthly, semi-annual, and annual), packages, and overnight stays. Memberships include 2 hours per day every day, totaling about 62 hours per month, at an economical rate per session.

Do you offer overnights?

Yes! Check-in is at 9pm, and check-out is at 7am for a total of 10 hours. You can sleep on our super-comfy lazyboy style reclining chairs. Please bring your own blanket.

How should I prepare for my visit?

Wear comfortable clothing and drink plenty of water. You may bring a blanket, eye mask, ear plugs, and a water bottle. You are welcome to bring crystals or a jug of water to charge and drink after your session.

What can I expect in and after the session?

Please arrive 10 minutes early, bring a water bottle, and turn off your phone. During the session, relax, close your eyes, and stay hydrated. Many feel energized but calm, with improved clarity and reduced pain.

How might I feel after a session?

People often feel relaxed but energized, with reduced pain and improved sleep. Detox symptoms may occur as toxins are released, so a detox bath is recommended.

How often would you suggest I have a session for maximum benefit?

People with health conditions may benefit from daily or weekly visits. Healthy individuals looking for a vitality boost might find monthly visits beneficial, though needs vary.

Are there any potential side effects or contraindications?

There are no known contraindications. For those with medical conditions, please consult your physician. Detox symptoms, such as those experienced after a massage, are normal and temporary.

How do I pay for a session?

We accept most forms of payment. You can book and pay online, over the phone, or in person at our center.

What if I need to cancel or change my booking?

We strive to be flexible. Please call us if you need to cancel or change your booking.

Are pets allowed?

Absolutely! Pets love the EESystem too. The charge for pets is $35 per hour. Overnight stays for pets are also available.

What are your hours of operation?

We are open Mon-Fri: 9am-7pm with the last session starting at 5pm and Sat-Sun: 9am-2pm with the last session starting at 12pm. Overnight check-ins are at 9pm and check-out at 7am.
