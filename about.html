What to Expect?

Before Your Session & Arrival: Please arrive 10 min early to fill out waivers for our complimentary membership to enter the EES room. It's very important you come hydrated & scent free. Bring a water bottle to keep hydrated ( filtered water refill station provided). Bring a blanket, pillow, preferred ear plugs and eye mask.

During your Session: After check-in please silence or turn off your phone completely. Feel free to use the restroom before your session so you may fully relax, close your eyes & fall asleep on the reclining chairs for maximum benefits and minimal interruptions. Focus on you & the purpose of your session. You may find yourself in a relaxed state and feel the time passes quickly.

After your Session: To assist the body in the detoxification process we recommend staying nourished & hydrated with minerals and electrolytes and taking a detox salt bath within 24 hours. You may also do a detox foot bath, sauna or going for a walk to support your bodies detox process.
